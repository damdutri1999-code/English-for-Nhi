## Danh sách `vocabList` (30 cụm: 18 mới + 12 cũ)

**18 cụm mới**:

- **Sub 1: Making a claim** (4 cụm): argue that, maintain that, there is no denying that, a compelling case
- **Sub 2: Supporting with evidence** (3 cụm): according to, a growing body of evidence, a case in point
- **Sub 3: Persuading the audience** (6 cụm): appeal to, call for, advocate, sway opinion, rally support, take a stand
- **Sub 4: Rebutting** (5 cụm): dismiss the claim, counter the argument, to some extent, beside the point, on the contrary

**12 cụm cũ quay lại** (`"recycled":true`, lấy từ tuần 1; gán 3 cụm cho mỗi sub 1 đến 4):

job displacement, labour market, skills gap, gig economy, boost productivity, a double-edged sword, widen the inequality gap, job security, reskill, lifelong learning, strike a balance, mitigate the impact

Mỗi cụm cũ có `ex` **mới** (khác ví dụ cũ) và được dùng lại trong bài đọc hoặc bài tập.
